# Three-Model Study Matrix v1 — Pile Retirement Amendment

**Status:** frozen planning amendment

**Execution authorized:** no

This amendment replaces the prospective evaluation binding in the unexecuted M1/M2 portion of the
three-model matrix from eval-v1 to eval-v2. Pile-10k is not scheduled at M0, M1, M2-A or M2-B and
cannot block selection or analysis. Every other matrix dependency, model identity, exact-prefix
requirement, M2 sibling rule, seed policy and matched-budget requirement remains unchanged.

Historical eval-v1 M0 configs and executions are preserved. The eval-v2 M0 input is a future
hash-closed projection of the 21 completed non-Pile lanes plus the completed exact-prefix panel;
this amendment does not run that projection or grant external execution.
