## 3. Main descriptive findings

### 3.1 The Turkish decline is directional and broad

The M2-clean minus M1 paired changes show the largest loss in TR→EN for both seeds:

| Direction | Seed 42 | Seed 43 |
|---|---:|---:|
| EN→EN | −1.24 pp | −3.00 pp |
| TR→EN | −18.74 pp | −18.83 pp |
| TR→TR | −6.59 pp | −6.87 pp |

Thus, the dominant M1→M2 degradation is not an English-retention failure. It is concentrated
in Turkish-involving retrieval/binding, especially the TR→EN direction, and is consistent
across both seeds.

### 3.2 Relation effects are heterogeneous, not a single-relation failure

The largest TR→EN M1→M2 losses include `profession` (−25.95 pp for seed 42 and −27.45 pp for
seed 43), `works_in_industry` (−22.23 and −23.93 pp), and `lives_in` (−20.57 and −18.73 pp).
The largest descriptive M3 recovery cells include `born_in` and `lives_in`, but recovery is
small relative to the preceding loss. This pattern points to heterogeneous relation
vulnerability rather than one relation accounting for the full directional effect.

### 3.3 M3-fact recovers only a modest fraction of the Turkish loss

Relative to M2-clean, M3-fact improves:

| Direction | Seed 42 | Seed 43 |
|---|---:|---:|
| TR→EN | +1.86 pp | +1.90 pp |
| TR→TR | +1.58 pp | +1.72 pp |

The recovery is descriptively consistent in sign, but it restores only roughly one tenth of the
TR→EN loss. It therefore does not erase the broad M2 Turkish degradation.

### 3.4 Direct versus QA and form patterns are secondary descriptive structure

Both direct and QA scaffolds decline in M2. For seed 42 the changes are −9.17 pp (direct) and
−8.54 pp (QA); for seed 43 they are −9.28 pp and −9.85 pp. M3-fact gives modest recovery in
both scaffolds: +1.01/+1.39 pp for seed 42 and +1.18/+1.70 pp for seed 43 (direct/QA).

All four forms show the same qualitative M2 decline and modest M3 recovery. The largest M2
form-level loss is form C (−10.15 pp for seed 42 and −11.27 pp for seed 43), while the
recovery range is approximately +1.02 to +1.54 pp for seed 42 and +1.31 to +1.52 pp for seed
43. These are descriptive contrasts, not evidence that form C or QA is the causal source of
the primary failure.

### 3.5 Branch A/B pattern does not establish the primary interaction

For seed 42, the M2 decline is −9.45 pp in Branch A and −8.26 pp in Branch B; for seed 43 it
is −9.54 pp and −9.59 pp. M3-fact recovery is +1.09 pp in Branch A for both seeds, compared
with +1.31 pp in Branch B for seed 42 and +1.79 pp for seed 43.

This is compatible with a descriptive Branch-B recovery pattern, especially for seed 43, but it
does not replace the frozen primary interaction test. The frozen interaction remains 0.0025
with 95% CI [−0.0051, 0.0101] for seed 42 and 0.0135 with 95% CI [0.0051, 0.0218] for seed
43. Consequently, the two-seed primary decision remains
`primary_success_criterion_not_met`.

## 4. Interpretation and limitations

The exploratory evidence supports a cautious mechanism description: generic Turkish adaptation
in M2 is associated with a broad Turkish-involving retrieval/binding decline, with additional
heterogeneity by relation, form, scaffold, and branch. The M3 factual arm recovers a small and
descriptively coherent portion of that loss, but the Branch-B-specific causal interaction is not
replicated as a passing result across both seeds.

These comparisons are post-hoc summaries of the frozen cells. They do not establish a causal
relation-level, form-level, scaffold-level, or branch-level explanation, and they cannot be used
to select another checkpoint or revise the primary gate.
